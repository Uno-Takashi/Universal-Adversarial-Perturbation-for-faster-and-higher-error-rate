## How many images does it need?

<MarginVsImages />

Every model starts **below** the random baseline and crosses it between 64 and 128 images. That
crossing point is the practical answer: below roughly 100 images this algorithm does not produce a
universal perturbation, it produces an overfitted one that is _less_ effective than noise of the same
magnitude.

## Absolute rates, with each model's floor

<FoolingVsImages />

The dashed lines show why the margin is the right statistic. Inception 5h sits at a 32–35% random
baseline while InceptionV3 sits at 11–17%: the same `val fool` means very different things on the
two. Older and lighter models are markedly more susceptible to plain noise.

## Fitted set versus held-out set

The gap between `gen fool` and `val fool` is the overfitting, and it closes as the generation set
grows — from 73.5 points at n=16 down to 11.7 points at n=1024 on MobileNetV2.

The clearest single case is InceptionV3 at 16 images: **100.0%** on the images it was fitted to,
**11.0%** on held-out images, against a 15.7% random floor. A perfect score on the fitted set and
worse than noise everywhere else.
